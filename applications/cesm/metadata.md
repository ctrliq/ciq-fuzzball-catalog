CESM (Community Earth System Model) – A fully-coupled global climate / Earth-system model developed by NCAR and the climate research community, used for CMIP-class climate projections, paleoclimate, idealized process studies, and large-ensemble experiments. Defaults run an F2000climo case (CAM6 atmosphere, CLM5 land, prescribed year-2000 SSTs) at 1.9° × 2.5° resolution for one simulated month.
